Learn more about the AfyaMicro concept and its proposed services.

AfyaMicro Kampala is a proposed digital health micro-insurance platform designed to help users prepare for healthcare expenses and manage their health wallet.

Visit the registration page and complete the requested personal information to create a demonstration account.

MTN Mobile Money and Airtel Money are presented as payment choices in the frontend demonstration. Actual payment processing is not connected.

Visit the pharmacy directory to explore the Kampala division categories. Listings should be confirmed before being treated as active partners.

THE AFYAMICRO EXPERIENCE

A more organised way to prepare for healthcare expenses.

AfyaMicro brings together useful account tools that help members understand and organise their healthcare-related financial activities through one digital experience.

Understand your finances

Use the wallet interface to view a demonstration balance, explore deposit options and review recorded wallet activity.

Keep your records organised

Explore contribution records, transaction history and account notifications from your member dashboard.

Explore healthcare information

Use the pharmacy directory to explore the planned regional directory structure and learn how participating providers may be presented.

Follow your account activity

Review account updates and submitted demonstration records through the member-facing pages.

Access information in one place

The separate pages are designed to make account information, wallet tools and healthcare guidance easier to navigate.

Use a responsive interface

The website is designed to adapt to desktop, tablet and mobile screens for a consistent browsing experience.

Important: AfyaMicro is currently a frontend demonstration. Displayed balances and payment records are simulated in the browser. The demonstration does not provide active insurance coverage, collect real payments or guarantee access to medical services.
