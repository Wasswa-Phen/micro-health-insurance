Who we are

A digital approach to healthcare preparation.

Healthcare expenses can arise unexpectedly. AfyaMicro Kampala is designed around the idea that preparing for healthcare costs should be easier to understand and manage.

◎
Our mission

Make healthcare preparation more accessible.

To develop an accessible digital platform that helps individuals organise healthcare contributions, monitor their health wallet and navigate participating pharmacy services.

◇
Our vision

A future where healthcare preparation is simpler.

To contribute to a future in which individuals and families can approach everyday healthcare expenses with greater awareness, organisation and confidence.

OUR PURPOSE

The challenge we want to address.

Healthcare expenses can be difficult to organise. AfyaMicro explores how a simple digital platform could help people keep track of healthcare-related financial information and provider information.

Better financial organisation

Provide a central interface for viewing wallet activity, contribution records and transaction information.

Accessible information

Present platform guidance and healthcare directory information in a format that is easier to understand and navigate.

Member visibility

Give members a dashboard concept where account details, submitted records and notifications can be reviewed.

Transparent communication

Clearly explain what each feature does, what information is recorded and which services are not yet connected.

Local relevance

Use Uganda-focused examples, Ugandan shilling amounts and Kampala division categories in the demonstration interface.

Room for future development

Establish a structured interface that can later be connected to properly implemented backend services and authorised partners.

Our current scope: AfyaMicro Kampala is a demonstration project, not a licensed insurance provider. Any future insurance product, coverage rules, provider agreements and payment services would require appropriate verification, authorisation and operational arrangements before launch.
