YOUR MEMBER JOURNEY

Understand each part of the platform.

Explore how the different areas of the AfyaMicro interface are intended to work together, from creating a member profile to reviewing account activity.

1. Create a member profile

Use the registration page to enter your name, Ugandan phone number, optional NIN and home pharmacy selection. The current demonstration stores limited profile information in browser storage.

2. Explore the wallet

Open the wallet page to view the demonstration balance, enter a permitted amount and select a displayed payment option.

3. Review contributions

Explore the contributions area to understand how contribution records and related account information are intended to be presented.

4. Explore pharmacy information

Browse the pharmacy directory and its division categories. Provider listings and partnership status must be verified before being treated as active service locations.

5. Submit a demonstration claim

Use the claims interface to explore the information requested when recording a claim, including service details and the amount submitted.

6. Track account activity

Review transactions, notifications and dashboard summaries to understand how member records are brought together.

Demonstration limitation: These steps explain the website interface only. Deposits do not transfer money, submitted claims are not sent to an insurer, and no insurance cover or medical benefit is activated by using this demonstration.
