MEMBER INFORMATION

Understanding your claim records

Use these guidelines when exploring the AfyaMicro claims demonstration.

Keep accurate records

Enter clear information about the healthcare expense, service date, provider and amount. Accurate sample entries make it easier to understand how a claims system could organise submissions.

Supporting documents

A real insurance process may require receipts, prescriptions or other supporting documents. This demonstration does not upload, verify or process those documents.

Understand claim status

A status displayed in this application is a sample record only. It does not mean an insurer or healthcare provider has reviewed or approved a claim.

Protect your privacy

Avoid entering real medical histories, sensitive personal information or confidential financial details into this demonstration.

Important reminder

AfyaMicro Kampala is currently a frontend demonstration. No claim is sent to an insurer, pharmacy or healthcare provider, and no reimbursement or medical benefit is provided.
