Demonstration and account safety

AfyaMicro Kampala is currently a frontend demonstration. Balances, contributions, claims and transactions are sample records stored in the browser. They do not provide active insurance coverage, process real payments or guarantee access to healthcare services. Avoid entering sensitive personal or medical information.
